# Research Data Management fundamentals

Research Data Management (RDM) is the set of practices used to collect, organise, document, store, share and preserve research data throughout a project and beyond.
Good RDM makes research more reproducible, reusable and compliant with funder and institutional requirements.

This page lists introductory courses that teach the fundamentals of RDM.
They are useful both for researchers who want to manage their own data better and for people who are starting to work as data stewards.

> [!NOTE]
> Adapted from a review of existing RDM courses compiled at Erasmus University Rotterdam (2026).
> Course details are paraphrased from the providers' pages; schedules, links and fees change over time.

## Netherlands

### Utrecht University
- :office: :beginner: [Introduction to Research Data Management (online, 2022 edition)](https://www.uu.nl/en/events/introduction-to-research-data-management-online-2022-i) — online, self-paced modules with platform chat over three weeks. The linked page refers to the 2022 edition; check the provider for current offerings.
    - **Core topics:** RDM and databases; data and models; MySQL; structuring and querying data; processing data with Python; data repositories.
    - **Learning objectives:** Understand the principles of RDM; model and structure research data; query databases using SQL; process data using Python; work with repositories and data schemas; improve data storage, reuse and compliance with RDM requirements.
    - **Exercises:** Online quizzes and assignments; a final assignment; practical work on data modelling, SQL queries, database creation, data integration and repository use.
    - **Resources:** Online platform, teaching materials and online trainer support.

### Leiden University
- :office: :beginner: [Training on research data management](https://www.library.universiteitleiden.nl/researchers/data-management/training-data-management) — a programme of online and in-person workshops of two to four hours each, with presentations, demonstrations and exercises.
    - **Core topics:** Writing a DMP; implementing FAIR data; RDM best practices; discipline-specific data management procedures; data storage; repository sharing and preservation; personal data; institutional archiving and repositories; linked open data.
    - **Learning objectives:** Manage research data according to disciplinary standards and best practices; develop a DMP; understand storage, sharing, preservation and archiving requirements; apply responsible research data practices.
    - **Exercises:** Hands-on exercises in each session; DMP drafting activities; discipline-specific practical work; data sharing and archiving activities.
    - **Resources:** Trainers, DMP templates, workshop materials and tutorials. Teaching materials are also published on [Zenodo](https://zenodo.org/communities/cds-teaching-material/records?q=&l=list&p=1&s=10&sort=newest) and [GitHub](https://github.com/cdsleiden).

### Delft University of Technology
- :office: :beginner: [Research Data Management 101](https://www.tudelft.nl/library/data-management/trainingen/trainingen-voor-onderzoekers-en/research-data-management-101) — a three-week blended course with self-paced online modules and three instructor-led sessions of two hours.
    - **Core topics:** RDM fundamentals; data types and research objects; funder and institutional requirements; FAIR principles and their implementation; research workflows; DMPs; developing a research data strategy.
    - **Learning objectives:** Understand the importance of good data management in research; identify different types of research data and the regulations, policies and legal requirements associated with them; list the main components of FAIR and connect them to your own research workflows; design an efficient RDM strategy according to best practices.
    - **Exercises:** Data flow map assignments; peer review activities; FAIR assessment of real datasets; documentation exercises; discussion forums; reflection on a personal RDM strategy.
    - **Resources:** Self-study module, videos, text lessons, quizzes, discussion forums, FAIR evaluation tools, instructor feedback and peer learning. The course is supported by the open [RDM101 book](https://tu-delft-library.github.io/rdm101-book/intro.html).

### Vrije Universiteit Amsterdam
- :office: :beginner: [PhD Research Data Management Kickstart course](https://vu.nl/en/about-vu/research-institutes/amsterdam-institute-of-molecular-and-life-sciences/more-about/research-data-management-aimms) — a three-hour introductory session followed by milestone activities across the PhD: DMP submission and review, two one-to-one consultations, and an RDM exit document needed to complete the course credit.
    - **Core topics:** Research data lifecycle; FAIR data; metadata; interoperability; archiving; DMPs; DMPonline.
    - **Learning objectives:** Understand key RDM concepts and practices; understand the FAIR data principles; become familiar with the research data lifecycle; develop a DMP; understand how RDM supports research quality and compliance.
    - **Exercises:** A practical exercise using DMPonline to create a DMP; DMP review and consultation.
    - **Resources:** Trainer and data steward support, course materials, consultation and feedback on the DMP.

### University of Amsterdam
- :office: :beginner: [Research Data Management (OPEN)](https://aihr.uva.nl/phds-in-the-humanities/phd-training/core-courses/research-data-management) — a one-day introductory workshop with pre-assignments.
    - **Core topics:** RDM fundamentals; research data lifecycle considerations; DMPs; data storage and management during the project; post-project data handling and preservation; RDM support services.
    - **Learning objectives:** Understand what research data are and what RDM involves; complete a DMP for your own research project; identify where to seek support for data management issues.
    - **Exercises:** Pre-assignments submitted before the workshop; completion of a DMP for your own project.
    - **Resources:** Trainer and data steward, workshop materials.

## Germany

### Technische Universität Berlin
- :office: :beginner: [E-learning on research data management](https://www.tu.berlin/en/ub/szf/training-consulting/e-learning-course) — online, self-paced, 90 to 120 minutes.
    - **Core topics:** What RDM is; planning RDM; collecting and storing research data; analysing and describing research data; archiving research data; sharing research data; reusing research data.
    - **Learning objectives:** Gain general knowledge about the various aspects of RDM and be able to apply fundamental RDM practices in your research.
    - **Exercises:** A quiz at the end of the course.
    - **Resources:** Online platform.

### University of Duisburg-Essen
- :office: :beginner: [Interdisciplinary Basics in Research Data Management](https://www.uni-due.de/rds/en/rdmcurriculum.php#basic) — a one-day in-person event, 9:00–16:00.
    - **Core topics:** RDM; FAIR; project organisation; data structure; tools.
    - **Learning objectives:** Learn what RDM is and why it is helpful for scientific work; understand the FAIR principles and how to apply them in practice; structure, organise and manage research data efficiently; work collaboratively with project partners; learn which tools make day-to-day data handling easier.
    - **Resources:** Teaching materials.

## Finland

### University of Turku
- :office: :beginner: [Basics of Research Data Management (3 ECTS)](https://opas.peppi.utu.fi/en/course/UGSL0002/100887) — online lectures and workshops, self-study modules in Moodle, several smaller assignments and a final assignment. The course runs once a year, around February or May.
    - **Core topics:** A detailed overview of RDM, with partly programme-specific teaching in four parallel tracks: Health Sciences, Survey Research, Qualitative Research and Natural Sciences. Planning and documentation; the research data lifecycle; intellectual property rights; data protection; data storage and security; data preservation; FAIR principles; selected data analysis software.
    - **Learning objectives:** Understand the relationship and differences between a research plan and a DMP, and produce a DMP for your own research; become familiar with data lifecycle thinking and the issues to address at each stage; understand your own data and its formats, and how those formats affect data management; identify opportunities for data reuse.
    - **Exercises:** Smaller assignments and a final assignment.
    - **Resources:** Online platform, online sessions and four programme-specific tracks.

### University of Eastern Finland
- :office: :beginner: [Basics of Research Data Management (1–2 ECTS)](https://sites.uef.fi/rdm/) — online self-study, open for half a year.
    - **Core topics:** Fundamentals of RDM and data management planning; legislation and ethical principles, including contracts, copyright, access rights and personal data; storing and processing data during research, including UEF digital services, storage solutions and data security; documentation and description, including metadata; sharing and opening research data; preserving and disposing of research data after the project; writing a DMP with DMPTuuli. An optional 1 ECTS module covers documentation and data description in more depth.
    - **Learning objectives:** Explain and exemplify what good RDM is and why it matters; illustrate good and responsible data management practices and apply them to your own data; apply your organisation's and other relevant guidelines to your own research data; write a DMP as part of a research plan and update it.
    - **Exercises:** Small assignments and a DMP.
    - **Resources:** An online module with credits, an online self-study version without credits, and an additional module for extra credits.

### Aalto University
- :office: :beginner: [Introduction to Research Data Management](https://www.aalto.fi/en/services/training-in-research-data-management-and-open-science) — an online seminar with a 40-minute presentation and a 50-minute interactive session.
    - **Core topics:** What RDM is; why it is useful; the stages of the data lifecycle; requirements from funders and publishers; services and support available at Aalto University.
    - **Learning objectives:** Understand what RDM entails; learn what to consider at each step of the data lifecycle; take practical steps towards FAIR data; find the services and support available at Aalto.
    - **Resources:** Zoom training, presentation and interactive session.

### University of Helsinki
- :office: :beginner: [RDM Basics](https://www.helsinki.fi/en/research/services-researchers/data-support/rdm-courses-workshops) — a two-hour online training.
    - **Core topics:** Basic concepts of RDM; what data is; ethical and legal compliance; data documentation; storage solutions from University of Helsinki IT Services; opening, publishing and archiving research data.
    - **Resources:** Online platform and teachers.
- :office: :arrow_forward: [RDM Advanced webinar series](https://www.helsinki.fi/en/research/services-researchers/data-support/rdm-courses-workshops) — four 1.5-hour webinars, each with a 30-minute introduction and a 60-minute Q&A.
    - **Core topics:** Key themes for effective and ethically sound RDM, elaborating on the topics introduced in RDM Basics. The series emphasises questions related to the Research Council of Finland's Winter call, whose applicants must outline a feasible DMP.
    - **Resources:** Online platform and teachers; introductory presentations and frequently asked questions are published afterwards, together with further reading.
