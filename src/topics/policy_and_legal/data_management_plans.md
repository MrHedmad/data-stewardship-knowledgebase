# Data Management Plans

A Data Management Plan (DMP) is a document that describes how research data will be handled during and after a project.
It usually covers data collection, documentation, storage, access, sharing, preservation and responsibilities, and is increasingly required by funders and research institutions.

This page lists courses that teach how to write, implement and maintain a DMP.

> [!NOTE]
> Adapted from a review of existing RDM courses compiled at Erasmus University Rotterdam (2026).
> Course details are paraphrased from the providers' pages; schedules, links and fees change over time.

## Netherlands

### Maastricht University
- :office: :beginner: [Introduction to Data Management Plans](https://library.maastrichtuniversity.nl/course/introduction-to-data-management-plans/) — a two-hour online training.
    - **Core topics:** The key aspects of creating, implementing and maintaining a successful DMP, through practical examples and hands-on exercises.
    - **Learning objectives:** Gain the knowledge needed to get started with DMPs; develop a clearer idea of RDM, including practices such as FAIR and their advantages; draft a DMP using a funder's template, with practical examples for real-world application.
    - **Resources:** Online training materials.

## Finland

### University of Helsinki
- :office: [DMP workshops](https://www.helsinki.fi/en/research/services-researchers/data-support/rdm-courses-workshops) — a hands-on workshop on best practices for writing a DMP.
    - **Core topics:** Every section of a DMP is presented with tips on what to write; time is reserved for starting a draft or working on an existing DMP; the latest funder templates available in DMPTuuli are used. The primary language is English, with guidance also available in Finnish.
    - **Learning objectives:** Start drafting a DMP, or improve an existing one, during the workshop.
    - **Resources:** Online platform, teachers and general DMP guidance.

## Related courses

Several general RDM courses also cover DMPs in detail:
