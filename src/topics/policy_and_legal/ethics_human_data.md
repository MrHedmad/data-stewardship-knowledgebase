# Ethics of Human Data

Research involving human participants raises ethical questions that go beyond legal compliance.
Informed consent, minimising harm, protecting vulnerable groups and submitting research to ethical review are central concerns for anyone collecting or reusing human data.

This page lists courses that cover research ethics and the responsible handling of human data.

> [!NOTE]
> Adapted from a review of existing RDM courses compiled at Erasmus University Rotterdam (2026).
> Course details are paraphrased from the providers' pages; schedules, links and fees change over time.

## Finland

### Aalto University
- :office: :information_desk_person: [Qualitative methods and research ethics review](https://www.aalto.fi/en/services/training-in-research-data-management-and-open-science) — an online seminar with a one-hour presentation and a 30-minute discussion.
    - **Core topics:** The ethical review process for research projects, focusing on qualitative methods and research with human participants. The Finnish TENK guidelines require ethical review for specific research designs, and scientific journals may require an ethical review statement for studies involving human participants. The seminar covers potential harm and risks to research participants, other individuals, the researcher and society; how to mitigate them by planning; informed consent in changing research questions and contexts; and good practices from previous studies.
    - **Learning objectives:** Learn from the experiences of studies reviewed by the Aalto ethics committee, with a practical approach to the issues and questions that commonly arise in qualitative research.
    - **Resources:** Zoom training, one-hour presentation and 30-minute discussion.

### Tampere University
- :office: [Ethical and responsible data management](https://libguides.tuni.fi/open-science-trainings/data-management) — a one-hour online training.
    - **Core topics:** What data management means at different stages of research and why it is an essential part of good scientific practice; ethical and responsible handling of research data; practical solutions for storing, archiving and sharing data during and after research.
    - **Learning objectives:** Manage research data ethically throughout the research lifecycle; make a DMP; understand the agreements, rights and permissions related to the use and creation of research data; identify common solutions and services for storing, archiving and sharing data; find additional instructions and know where to ask for help.
    - **Resources:** One-hour online training.
