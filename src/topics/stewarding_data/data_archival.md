# Data Archival

Data archival is the process of preparing research data for long-term preservation and making it available for future reuse.
It involves choosing suitable repositories, documenting the data well enough for others to understand it, and planning for storage, access and, where necessary, disposal.

This page lists courses that cover data storage, repository deposit and long-term preservation.

> [!NOTE]
> Adapted from a review of existing RDM courses compiled at Erasmus University Rotterdam (2026).
> Course details are paraphrased from the providers' pages; schedules, links and fees change over time.

## Netherlands

### Maastricht University
- :office: [DataverseNL in Practice: Requirements, Workflows, and Best Practices](https://library.maastrichtuniversity.nl/course/dataversenl-in-practice-requirements-workflows-and-best-practices/) — an online training.
    - **Core topics:** How DataverseNL works from both the depositor and curator perspectives; the required and recommended steps for dataset deposit; the review process; options for controlling access to and reuse of research data; metadata quality; handling sensitive or anonymised data; applying licences; updating dataset versions; workflows, technical limitations and compliance requirements.
    - **Learning objectives:** Learn Maastricht University's requirements and recommended practices for depositing datasets in DataverseNL, including metadata quality, anonymisation and file format guidelines; examine the dataset review process from depositor and curator perspectives, including feedback handling, access controls and publication options; examine features such as embargo periods, restricted files and custom terms of use for sensitive data; update and link datasets in compliance with DataverseNL policies, including versioning rules and integration with external platforms.
    - **Resources:** Online training materials.

## Finland

### Aalto University
- :office: :arrow_forward: [Data storage and management](https://www.aalto.fi/en/services/training-in-research-data-management-and-open-science) — a webinar.
    - **Core topics:** Effective management of data throughout its lifecycle to optimise storage, ensure security and maximise the ability to reuse data; general principles for selecting storage services and best practices for work continuity, efficient data management and reusability; good data management routines and their benefits; questions to consider at the beginning of a project, group data storage, removing unnecessary data and preserving data; examples of applying these principles with Aalto University storage services; accessibility, long-term availability and confidentiality; cross-organisational collaboration.
    - **Learning objectives:** Learn the principles for arranging research data and storage; learn about the data storage options available at Aalto University; learn how to select the most suitable storage system for different needs.
    - **Resources:** Webinar recording.
