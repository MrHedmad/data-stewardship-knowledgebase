# Project planning

Good data management starts before any data are collected.
Planning how data will be collected, structured, stored, documented and shared helps teams avoid duplicated work, lost files and compliance problems later in a project.

This page lists courses that help research groups and projects plan their data management and set up data collection.

> [!NOTE]
> Adapted from a review of existing RDM courses compiled at Erasmus University Rotterdam (2026).
> Course details are paraphrased from the providers' pages; schedules, links and fees change over time.

## Planning data management in teams

### Technische Universität Berlin
- :office: [RDM Coaching for Research Groups](https://www.tu.berlin/en/ub/szf/training-consulting/rdm-coaching-for-research-groups) — at least four sessions of 120 minutes each, including a 60-minute introductory meeting online, held in person with a research group.
    - **Core topics:** Any aspect of team-based data management, such as storage locations, version control, data structure, documentation, archiving, backup, data publication, responsibilities and handover processes.
    - **Learning objectives:** Example outcomes include checklists for onboarding and offboarding team members; a shared folder structure; backup rules for the group based on the 3-2-1 principle; templates for README files; strategies for data publication or archiving; and defined data management roles within the team.
    - **Exercises:** The team develops its own solution during the sessions.
    - **Resources:** Teaching materials and Microsoft 365 Teams for the introductory meeting.

## Collecting new data

### University of Helsinki
- :office: [REDCap Training Sessions](https://www.helsinki.fi/en/research/services-researchers/data-support/rdm-courses-workshops) — a two-hour online training.
    - **Core topics:** An introduction to REDCap and its features; basic functions and creating a survey; issues to pay attention to when collecting sensitive data. No previous experience is needed.
    - **Learning objectives:** Gain a baseline competency in REDCap.
    - **Resources:** Online platform and teachers.
