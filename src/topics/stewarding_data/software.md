# Stewarding software

Modern research depends on software: scripts for data processing, analysis notebooks, simulation code and the tools used to manage and publish data.
Stewarding software means treating it as a research output that needs to be versioned, documented, tested, shared and preserved, much like data itself.

This page lists courses that cover research software and the digital tools used to manage data.

> [!NOTE]
> Adapted from a review of existing RDM courses compiled at Erasmus University Rotterdam (2026).
> Course details are paraphrased from the providers' pages; schedules, links and fees change over time.

## Netherlands

### Radboud University
- :office: [Develop your research skills: data management and research software](https://www.ru.nl/en/staff/researchers/support-and-development/trainings) — a modular programme of workshops of one to two hours each.
    - **Core topics:** DMPs; GDPR and personal data; data anonymisation; research data organisation; metadata and documentation; audio and video data collection; repositories; data archiving and publication. Additional modules cover programming, text mining, Git, generative AI and other digital research skills.
    - **Learning objectives:** Learn to organise, document, protect, manage, archive and publish research data; understand privacy requirements; create DMPs; improve data reusability and accessibility; develop good data management practices throughout the research lifecycle.
    - **Exercises:** Hands-on exercises such as DMP writing sessions, repository use, metadata creation, data organisation strategies, anonymisation approaches and archiving workflows. Many workshops apply the concepts to participants' own projects.
    - **Resources:** Trainers, the institutional repository and workshop materials.

## Germany

### University of Duisburg-Essen
- :office: [RDM Advanced workshops](https://www.uni-due.de/rds/en/rdmcurriculum.php#advanced) — a series of online workshops of two hours each.
    - **Core topics:** Examples of advanced workshops include data reuse and reusable data; Coscine, a platform for storing, sharing and archiving data; describing and documenting data; DMPs; a hands-on introduction to eLabFTW; an introduction to Python; "From Zero to Open Science Hero"; copyright and Creative Commons; project management in GitLab; FAIR and qualitative data; and reproducible analysis in R Notebooks.
    - **Learning objectives:** Learn about specialised topics in RDM and how to use specialised tools.
    - **Resources:** Teaching materials.
